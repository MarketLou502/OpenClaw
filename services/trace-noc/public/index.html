Commands Sheet

            
Voice & iMessage commands that bypass Main

            

              These phrasings never touch an LLM. iMessage and the Home Assistant Voice PE
              adapter both hand every message to shared-routine-router first — a
              grammar matcher (hassil, via recognize.py) that checks the text
              against a fixed set of sentence patterns per domain. A match calls a
              deterministic workflow script directly (dashboard-workflow.js,
              calendar-workflow.js, health-workflow.js,
              finance-workflow.js) and replies immediately. Anything that
              doesn't match — including every phrasing not shown below — falls through to
              Main, which delegates conversationally to the right specialist instead.
              Source: services/shared-routine-router/index.js and
              sentences/en/*.yaml, read directly for this sheet on 2026-08-10.
            

            

              Global escape hatch — any sentence ending in
              "…never mind" is caught before grammar matching even runs and replies
              "OK, never mind." with no agent involved at all.

Task Tracker — Calendar, Work / Personal / Market Lou, daily habits

              
Merged 2026-08-10 from three former specialists — Scheduler, Boards, and Goals — into one agent. Calendar ops run calendar-workflow.js directly (no confirmation step — a matched add/delete/reschedule executes immediately); board and habit ops run dashboard-workflow.js against the work/personal/market-lou boards and the daily-habits store.

            

            

              
Calendar: Add"Add team meeting to my calendar for tomorrow at 9am"

              
Calendar: List"What do I have scheduled for today?" · "Show me my calendar for Friday"

              
Calendar: Delete"Remove team meeting from my calendar"

              
Calendar: Reschedule"Move team meeting from today to tomorrow at 3pm"

              
Boards: Add"Add call the dentist to my work board"

              
Boards: List"What's on my personal board?" · "Show me all my three boards"

              
Boards: Complete"Mark call the dentist done on my work board" · "I just finished call the dentist" (free-form — searches boards, habits, and lists for a unique match)

              
Boards: Remove"Delete call the dentist from my personal board"

              
Boards: Schedule / reschedule / unschedule"Schedule call the dentist on my work board for tomorrow at 2pm" · "Move … to Friday at 10am" · "Unschedule call the dentist on my work board"

              
Habits: List"Show me my daily goals" · "Did I do all my habits today?"

              
Habits: Complete"Mark meditate done on my habits"

            

            
⚠ A calendar time is required (am/pm or 24h HH:MM); a missing date defaults to today, not "someday." "What's on my calendar" and similar query phrasings are also defined as a separate QueryCalendar intent in the grammar (calendar.yaml) — but as of this reading, index.js's router switch only has a wired case for ListCalendar, not QueryCalendar. If the recognizer picks the unwired intent for a given phrasing, that turn falls through to Main instead of bypassing it. Worth re-verifying live before relying on exact query wording. "Accenture" and "Market" are also accepted aliases for the work / Market Lou boards. As of 2026-08-10, the six daily habits no longer place calendar events — Task Tracker's off-calendar planner seeds sparse "coming up" text reminders instead; only task-linked due dates still go on the calendar.

Lists — custom saved lists

              
Also dashboard-workflow.js, against tasks/lists.json.

            

            

              
Create"Create a new list called Books to Read"

              
List all"What custom lists do I have?"

              
Add item"Add Project Hail Mary to my Books to Read list"

              
Show"What's on my Books to Read list?"

              
Complete item"Mark Project Hail Mary done on my Books to Read list"

            

            
⚠ Renaming/deleting a whole list, and editing/removing a single item by itself, were deliberately pulled out of the router (RenameList/DeleteList/EditListItem/RemoveListItem all hit an explicit "removed — handled by the model-based agent" bail in index.js). Those always go conversationally through Main → Lists, even if phrased exactly like the grammar above.

Health Tracker — workouts, runs, food log

              
Runs health-workflow.js directly for logging/queries. Food reports get their own fast path below.

            

            

              
Log workout"I just finished my workout" · "Log a workout"

              
Log run"I just went for a run" · "Log my daily run"

              
List food"What did I eat today?" · "Show me my food log for yesterday"

              
Activity"How active was I today?"

              
Correct entry"Fix my food entry for today"

              
Remove entry"Delete my last food entry"

              
Undo"Undo that" · "Never mind about that food entry"

            

            

              
Food fast path (cross-cutting — no verb required)

              

                
Report food"I had oatmeal with a banana" · "I just ate two eggs" · "Log a turkey sandwich"

              

              
Tier 1: exact match against your saved recipes (shared with Meal Planner). Tier 2: a USDA nutrition-index lookup with portion-size parsing ("half a cup of yogurt" → ~122g). Below-confidence or unmatched items — a branded item like "a large sweet tea from McDonald's" — fall through to Main → Health Tracker instead of logging blind.

Sub-agents by tool access

              
Read directly from openclaw.json's agents.list on 2026-08-10. Every agent gets the full OpenClaw tool catalog by default; each row lists what's denied for that agent specifically. group:* tags are OpenClaw framework tool-bundles (web = browser/web_fetch/web_search; ui = image/canvas-type tools; memory = memory_get; sessions = session_status/sessions_history/sessions_send/sessions_spawn/sessions_list/sessions_yield; nodes = nodes/node_inference; agents = agents_list/subagents) — exact membership is defined by the framework, not this repo, so treat that decoding as best-effort.
