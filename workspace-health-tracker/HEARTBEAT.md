# HEARTBEAT.md - Disabled

Health Tracker has no model-driven heartbeat and sends no proactive messages.
Do not send calorie, protein, missed-run, end-of-day, or extra workout nudges.

Main's deterministic native-iMessage workflow sends the only approved health
notification: one hourly workout prompt at each of eight slots from 9:00 AM
through 4:00 PM. Health Tracker only processes delegated logging requests.
